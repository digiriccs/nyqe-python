Our Story

Founded with a vision to transform the wealth management landscape in India, NYQE Wealth Management was born from the understanding that traditional financial advisory services were often inaccessible, complex, and expensive for the average investor.

Our founders, with decades of combined experience in finance, technology, and client service, recognized the need for a modern approach to wealth management—one that combines the personal touch of traditional advisory with the efficiency and accessibility of modern technology.

Today, we're proud to serve hundreds of clients across India, helping them navigate their financial journeys with confidence and achieve their wealth creation goals through disciplined investing and strategic planning.
