Cookie Policy

Understanding how we use cookies and tracking technologies

Home > Cookie Policy
Last Updated: December 29, 2024

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit our website. They contain information about your browsing activity and preferences, helping us provide you with a better, more personalized experience.

NYQE Wealth Management uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website, analyze usage patterns, and improve our services. This Cookie Policy explains how we use these technologies and your choices regarding them.

🍪 Cookie Consent

By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings or using our cookie preference center.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Classification by Purpose

Cookie Type Purpose Examples Consent Required
Essential Enable basic website functionality, security, and navigation Login sessions, security tokens, load balancing No - Legally required
Functional Remember your preferences and provide enhanced features Language selection, calculator settings, font size Yes
Analytics Understand how visitors interact with our website Page views, click tracking, user journey analysis Yes
Marketing Deliver personalized content and measure advertising effectiveness Ad targeting, conversion tracking, remarketing Yes

2.2 Classification by Duration

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser. Used for maintaining your session during a single visit.
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a specified period or until manually deleted. Used to remember your preferences across multiple visits.

2.3 Classification by Source

  • First-Party Cookies: Set directly by NYQE Wealth Management for our website functionality and analytics.
  • Third-Party Cookies: Set by external services integrated into our website, such as analytics and advertising platforms.

4. How We Use Cookies

4.1 Website Functionality

  • Authentication: Keeping you logged in during your session and remembering your login preferences
  • Security: Protecting against fraudulent activities and maintaining secure connections
  • Navigation: Remembering your position within multi-step processes and forms
  • Load Balancing: Distributing traffic across our servers for optimal performance
  • Error Prevention: Preventing cross-site request forgery and other security threats

4.2 Personalization

  • User Preferences: Storing your language, theme, and accessibility preferences
  • Calculator Settings: Remembering your inputs in financial calculators for convenience
  • Dashboard Customization: Maintaining your preferred layout and widget arrangements
  • Content Personalization: Showing relevant content based on your interests and behavior
  • Notification Preferences: Respecting your choices about communication frequency and channels

4.3 Analytics & Performance

  • Usage Analytics: Understanding which pages and features are most popular
  • Performance Monitoring: Identifying slow-loading pages and technical issues
  • User Journey Analysis: Tracking how visitors navigate through our website
  • Conversion Tracking: Measuring the effectiveness of our content and calls-to-action
  • A/B Testing: Comparing different versions of pages to improve user experience
  • Error Tracking: Identifying and fixing technical problems quickly

4.4 Marketing & Communication

  • Ad Personalization: Showing relevant advertisements based on your interests
  • Conversion Attribution: Understanding which marketing channels drive the most engagement
  • Remarketing: Re-engaging visitors who have shown interest in our services
  • Social Media Integration: Enabling social sharing and tracking social media referrals
  • Email Marketing: Coordinating website behavior with email marketing campaigns
  • Cross-Device Tracking: Providing consistent experience across different devices

6. Your Cookie Choices & Controls

6.1 Cookie Consent Management

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner that allows you to:

Cookie Consent Example

We use cookies to enhance your experience. Choose your preferences:

  • Accept All: Consent to all cookie types for the best possible experience
  • Decline All: Only essential cookies will be used (may limit functionality)
  • Customize: Choose specific cookie categories based on your preferences

6.2 Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

6.3 Opt-Out Tools

  • Google Analytics Opt-out: Browser Add-on
  • Digital Advertising Alliance: Industry Opt-out Page
  • Network Advertising Initiative: Opt-out Tool
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance: Your Online Choices

6.4 Mobile App Controls

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Advertising → Limit Ad Tracking
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • App Settings: Privacy controls within our mobile application

6.5 Impact of Disabling Cookies

Disabling certain cookies may affect your experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Website may not function properly
  • Functional Cookies: Loss of personalized settings and preferences
  • Analytics Cookies: We cannot improve our services based on usage data
  • Marketing Cookies: Less relevant advertisements and content
