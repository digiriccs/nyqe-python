1. Introduction & Commitment

NYQE Wealth Management Private Limited ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you use our website, mobile applications, or avail our investment advisory services.

This Privacy Policy applies to all personal information collected by NYQE Wealth Management through our digital platforms, physical offices, and during the provision of our services. By using our services or providing your personal information, you consent to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

🔒 Our Privacy Commitment

We adhere to the highest standards of data protection as mandated by Indian laws including the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Personal Data Protection Bill. Your trust is paramount to our business.

5. Data Security Measures

5.1 Technical Security Controls

  • Encryption: 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission and AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Access Controls: Role-based access control (RBAC) with least privilege principle
  • Authentication: Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all user accounts and administrative access
  • Network Security: Firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and network segmentation
  • Database Security: Database encryption, activity monitoring, and access logging
  • Application Security: Secure coding practices, regular security testing, and vulnerability assessments
  • Endpoint Protection: Anti-malware, device encryption, and remote wipe capabilities

5.2 Physical Security Measures

  • Data Centers: Tier III certified data centers with 24/7 physical security
  • Office Security: Biometric access controls, CCTV surveillance, and visitor management
  • Document Security: Secure storage of physical documents in locked filing systems
  • Equipment Security: Asset tracking, secure disposal, and clean desk policies
  • Environmental Controls: Temperature, humidity, and fire suppression systems

5.3 Organizational Security Measures

  • Employee Training: Regular cybersecurity awareness and data protection training
  • Background Checks: Comprehensive screening of all employees and contractors
  • Confidentiality Agreements: All staff sign strict confidentiality and data protection agreements
  • Incident Response: 24/7 security operations center and incident response team
  • Business Continuity: Disaster recovery and business continuity planning
  • Third-party Management: Vendor risk assessment and ongoing monitoring

5.4 Monitoring & Auditing

  • Real-time Monitoring: 24/7 security monitoring and threat detection
  • Audit Logging: Comprehensive logging of all system access and data operations
  • Regular Audits: Annual information security audits by certified professionals
  • Vulnerability Management: Regular security assessments and penetration testing
  • Compliance Monitoring: Continuous monitoring for regulatory compliance
  • Security Metrics: Key performance indicators for security effectiveness

5.5 Data Breach Response

In the event of a data breach, we have established procedures to:

  • Detect and contain the breach within 24 hours
  • Assess the scope and impact of the incident
  • Notify affected individuals within 72 hours where required
  • Report to relevant authorities as mandated by law
  • Implement corrective measures to prevent recurrence
  • Provide support and assistance to affected individuals

7. Your Privacy Rights

7.1 Access Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access Your Data: Request a copy of all personal information we hold about you
  • Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Processing Information: Understand how your data is being processed and for what purposes
  • Third-party Sharing: Know which third parties have access to your information
  • Data Sources: Information about where we obtained your data

7.2 Control Rights

  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information
  • Restriction: Limit the processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing where applicable
  • Marketing Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time

7.3 Erasure Rights

You may request deletion of your personal data when:

  • The data is no longer necessary for the original purpose
  • You withdraw consent and there's no other legal basis for processing
  • The data has been unlawfully processed
  • Erasure is required for compliance with legal obligations

Note: Some data cannot be deleted due to legal and regulatory requirements.

9.1 Parental Consent Requirements
