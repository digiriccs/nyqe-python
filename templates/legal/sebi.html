2. Investment Risk Warnings

CRITICAL RISK DISCLOSURE

All investments carry inherent risks and are subject to market volatility. The value of investments can go up or down, and investors may receive back less than the amount originally invested. Past performance is not a guarantee of future results.

2.1 General Investment Risks

  • Market Risk: Fluctuations in security prices due to market conditions, economic factors, and investor sentiment
  • Credit Risk: Risk of default by issuers of debt securities, affecting bond and fixed income investments
  • Interest Rate Risk: Impact of interest rate changes on bond prices and fixed income securities
  • Liquidity Risk: Difficulty in selling investments quickly at fair market value
  • Inflation Risk: Erosion of purchasing power due to rising prices over time
  • Currency Risk: Exchange rate fluctuations affecting international investments
  • Political Risk: Government policy changes, regulatory modifications, and political instability
  • Concentration Risk: Over-exposure to specific sectors, companies, or geographic regions

2.2 Mutual Fund Specific Risks

  • Scheme Specific Risk: Risks associated with the investment strategy and portfolio composition of specific mutual fund schemes
  • AMC Risk: Risks related to the fund management company's financial health and management quality
  • Benchmark Risk: Possibility of fund performance deviating significantly from its benchmark index
  • Exit Load: Charges applicable on premature redemption of mutual fund units
  • Tax Implications: Changes in tax laws affecting mutual fund returns and investor liabilities
  • Tracking Error: Deviation of fund performance from its underlying index (for index funds)

2.3 Equity Investment Risks

  • Price Volatility: High fluctuation in stock prices due to company-specific and market factors
  • Company Risk: Business risks, management changes, and corporate governance issues
  • Sectoral Risk: Industry-specific challenges and regulatory changes
  • Small & Mid-Cap Risk: Higher volatility and liquidity constraints in smaller companies
  • Dividend Risk: Uncertainty in dividend payments and policy changes

2.4 Fixed Income Investment Risks

  • Interest Rate Sensitivity: Inverse relationship between interest rates and bond prices
  • Credit Rating Risk: Downgrade of issuer's credit rating affecting bond values
  • Reinvestment Risk: Risk of reinvesting coupon payments at lower interest rates
  • Call Risk: Early redemption by issuers when interest rates decline
  • Duration Risk: Price sensitivity to interest rate changes based on bond maturity

3. SEBI Code of Conduct Compliance

3.1 Fiduciary Responsibilities

As a SEBI registered Investment Adviser, we adhere to the highest fiduciary standards:

  • Client Interest Priority: Always acting in the best interest of our clients
  • Professional Competence: Maintaining high standards of professional knowledge and skills
  • Integrity and Fairness: Conducting business with integrity, fairness, and transparency
  • Confidentiality: Maintaining strict confidentiality of client information
  • Disclosure of Conflicts: Full disclosure of any potential conflicts of interest
  • Reasonable Care: Exercising reasonable care and due diligence in providing advice

3.3 Prohibited Activities

As per SEBI regulations, we do not engage in:

  • Guaranteed Returns: Promising or guaranteeing specific investment returns
  • Fund Management: Managing client funds or securities directly
  • Broking Activities: Executing trades or acting as a broker
  • Margin Trading: Providing facilities for margin trading or derivatives
  • Insider Trading: Trading on material non-public information
  • Market Manipulation: Engaging in activities that manipulate security prices
  • Misleading Communications: Making false or misleading statements about investments

4. Client Responsibilities & Suitability

4.1 Client Due Diligence

Clients are responsible for:

  • Accurate Information: Providing complete and accurate financial information, including income, assets, liabilities, and investment experience
  • Risk Assessment: Honestly assessing their risk tolerance and investment capacity
  • Investment Objectives: Clearly communicating their financial goals, time horizons, and investment preferences
  • Regular Updates: Informing us of any material changes in financial circumstances or investment objectives
  • Document Review: Carefully reading and understanding all investment-related documents before making decisions
  • Independent Decision Making: Making final investment decisions based on their own judgment and circumstances

4.2 Suitability Framework

  • Risk Capacity: Ability to bear investment losses based on financial situation
  • Risk Tolerance: Psychological comfort level with investment volatility
  • Investment Horizon: Time period for which investments can be held
  • Liquidity Needs: Requirements for accessible funds for emergencies or planned expenses
  • Tax Considerations: Impact of tax implications on investment returns
  • Investment Knowledge: Understanding of different investment products and their risks

4.3 Ongoing Responsibilities

  • Portfolio Monitoring: Regularly reviewing investment performance and portfolio allocation
  • Market Awareness: Staying informed about market conditions and economic developments
  • Rebalancing: Implementing recommended portfolio rebalancing when advised
  • Tax Planning: Coordinating investment strategies with tax planning requirements
  • Goal Tracking: Monitoring progress toward financial goals and adjusting strategies as needed

Our fee structure complies with SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations:

  • Fee-Only Model: We charge advisory fees only from clients, not from product manufacturers

  • Performance Fees: Any performance-based fees comply with SEBI guidelines and are clearly disclosed
