Capital Gains Tax Optimization

Strategic planning to minimize LTCG and STCG tax burden

Long Term Capital Gains (LTCG)
Holding Period: More than 1 year
Tax Rate: 10% above ₹1 lakh
Exemption: ₹1 lakh per year
Our Optimization Strategies:
  • Annual ₹1 lakh exemption utilization
  • Strategic profit booking timing
  • Tax loss harvesting opportunities
  • Reinvestment in growth assets
Short Term Capital Gains (STCG)
Holding Period: Less than 1 year
Tax Rate: 15% on all gains
Exemption: Nil
Our Optimization Strategies:
  • Extend holding period to qualify for LTCG
  • Offset gains with short-term losses
  • Time entries and exits strategically
  • Focus on dividend-paying stocks
